Our Story

Tanzania's Trusted Technology Partner

TESTECH Company Ltd was founded with a clear mission: to bring world-class technology and engineering solutions to businesses across East Africa. Since our inception, we have grown from a small team of passionate engineers into one of Tanzania's most respected technology firms.

We believe that quality and reliability are not trade-offs — they are the standard. Every project we undertake, regardless of size, receives the same level of professional attention and commitment to excellence.

Today, TESTECH serves over 300 clients across Tanzania and 5 East African countries, with a portfolio spanning IT infrastructure, electrical engineering, security systems, telecommunications, and ongoing maintenance services.
